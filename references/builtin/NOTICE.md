## RRUFF Project spectra (the `.txt` files)

Unmodified `Raman_Data_Processed` samples from the **RRUFF Project** mineral
Raman database (University of Arizona).

- Source: https://rruff.info/ (also served as https://www.rruff.net/)
- Copyright: RRUFF Project / University of Arizona.
- Citation: Lafuente B, Downs R T, Yang H, Stone N (2015) The power of
  databases: the RRUFF project. In: Highlights in Mineralogical
  Crystallography, T Armbruster and R M Danisi, eds. Berlin, Germany,
  W. De Gruyter, pp 1-30.

RRUFF publishes these spectra for public download but does not attach an
explicit license or terms-of-use statement to them. They are therefore
included here on the understanding that they are made publicly available for
research and educational use, with the citation above. No warranty is made
about their suitability for any other purpose, and no additional rights are
granted or implied by this repository's license. If you intend to redistribute
this data or use it commercially, confirm the terms with RRUFF directly.

These few files exist so the **Match** feature works on first run without any
download. For broader coverage, use **Match → Library → Download RRUFF
subset** in the app, or drop your own RRUFF / two-column CSV files into the
parent `references/` folder and rebuild the index. Anything downloaded that
way is likewise third-party data under its originator's terms.

## Ibuprofen peak-list reference (the `.csv` file)

`Ibuprofenum (Ibuprofen) literature peak reference.csv` is not a raw RRUFF
spectrum. It is a peak list compiled from published literature values, with
qualitative relative intensities used as matching weights. The source
publications and DOIs are cited in the file's own header.
